Poster at the 2026 annual meeting of the SIAM DC–Maryland–Virginia Section, held at the FUSE Building on George Mason University's Arlington Campus. The poster presented a convex-duality framework for flux-constrained flow in porous media, with analysis and finite element numerics. Joint work with H. Antil and K. L. A. Kirk. Related preprint: [arXiv:2607.13256](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13256).
