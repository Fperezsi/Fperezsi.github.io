Talk at the Fall 2026 Finite Element Circus, hosted at the University of Connecticut's Avery Point Campus. I presented a convex-duality framework for flux-constrained flow in porous media, covering both the analysis and the finite element numerics. Joint work with H. Antil and K. L. A. Kirk. Related preprint: [arXiv:2607.13256](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13256).
